Chronic code cases

A chronic address is one where the city opened 3 or more code cases (open and closed) in about two years.

at least 3

Chronic-only = only repeat minor complaints (grass, trash, curb items), no serious case and no other lead type.

Where this data comes from

Computed in-tool (analysis, not a source): lead score and tier, deal-path fit, and the owner and property flags. A lead found under two or more lead types is stacked.
Every lead is sourced from official government or statutory records. Each lead type is listed under the property, with a link to its public record where the county publishes one.
